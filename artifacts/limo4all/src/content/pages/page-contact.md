### Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will my inquiry be answered?

We respond to all inquiries within one hour during business hours. Outside business hours, we respond within two hours. For urgent matters, call our 24/7 emergency line.

What information do I need to provide for a quote?

For an accurate quote, provide your pickup location, destination, preferred vehicle type, date, and time. Any special requests help us customize your quote.

Can I book same-day transportation?

Yes. Our 24/7 service accommodates last-minute bookings. Call or use our app for immediate reservations.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

Contact us immediately with any changes. We accommodate rescheduling and cancellations based on our flexible policies outlined in our Terms and Conditions.

Do you serve locations outside Toronto?

Yes. We serve all 16 cities in our service area and provide worldwide transportation through our trusted partner network.

Is there a minimum booking requirement?

No. We accommodate single rides and recurring service equally well. Whether it's a one-time trip or ongoing corporate transportation, we're ready to serve.

How do I pay for my transportation?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, digital payments, and corporate billing. Add your payment method during booking or pay your driver directly.
