# Privacy Policy

## Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Limo4All Transportation is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring complete transparency about how we collect, use, and protect your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your data and your rights as our customer.

Last Updated: March 2026
Effective Date: March 15, 2026

### 1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide professional transportation services and improve your experience. Information collection is limited to what's required for service delivery and legal compliance.

Personal Identification Information

When you book transportation or contact us, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and physical address. This information is essential for confirming your reservation and contacting you about your ride.

Payment Information

When you pay for services, we collect payment details including credit card information, billing address, and transaction history. Payment processing is handled by PCI-DSS compliant third-party processors. We never store full credit card numbers on our servers.

Pickup and Destination Information

We collect information about your pickup location, destination, date, time, and vehicle preferences. This information is used exclusively to coordinate your transportation and improve route planning.

Driver and Vehicle Preferences

We may record your preferences for drivers, vehicle types, temperature settings, music preferences, and special requests. These preferences help us personalize your experience on future rides.

Device and Usage Information

When you use our mobile app or website, we collect information about your device type, operating system, browser type, IP address, and how you interact with our platform. This helps us optimize our booking systems and mobile applications.

Location Information

With your explicit permission, we collect real-time location data while you're using our app to facilitate driver pickup and tracking. Location tracking stops when your ride is complete unless you consent to continued tracking.

Communication Records

We maintain records of communications you send us via email, phone, or chat. These records help us resolve issues and improve our customer service.

Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third parties including corporate accounts, event planners, or hotel concierge services coordinating your transportation. We treat this information with the same privacy protections as directly collected information.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to remember your preferences, maintain security, and analyze site usage. You can control cookie settings through your browser, though some features may be limited if cookies are disabled.

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for legitimate business purposes directly related to transportation services and customer support.

Service Delivery

Your information is used to confirm reservations, coordinate drivers and vehicles, process payments, provide real-time tracking, and deliver transportation services. This is our primary use of your data.

Communication

We use your contact information to send reservation confirmations, ride reminders, driver arrival notifications, and service updates. We only send marketing communications with your explicit consent.

Customer Support

Your information helps us respond to questions, resolve disputes, handle complaints, and improve our customer service quality. Support communications are confidential and handled professionally.

Safety and Security

We use your information to verify identities, prevent fraud, maintain insurance compliance, conduct background checks on drivers, and ensure vehicle safety. Your safety is our paramount concern.

Legal and Regulatory Compliance

We use information as required by law, government requests, court orders, or regulatory obligations. We disclose the minimum information necessary to comply with legal requirements.

Service Improvement

We analyze usage patterns and feedback to improve our booking systems, route planning, vehicle maintenance, driver training, and overall service quality. Analysis is conducted with anonymized data when possible.

Fraud Prevention

We monitor for suspicious activity to prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and misuse of our systems. Fraud prevention protects both your account and our business integrity.

Corporate Account Management

For corporate clients, we track usage for billing purposes, account management, and service customization. Corporate account information is subject to additional confidentiality agreements.

### 3. Information Sharing

We protect your information and share it only when necessary for service delivery or legal compliance.

Service Providers and Partners

We share your pickup and destination information with drivers assigned to your ride. Drivers access only information necessary to complete your transportation. We share payment information with PCI-compliant payment processors. We partner with third-party vendors for app hosting, email delivery, and customer support, subject to strict data protection agreements.

Corporate Accounts and Coordination Services

If you're booked through a corporate account, we share ride details with designated corporate account managers. If coordinated through a hotel, event planner, or similar service, we share relevant booking information with the coordinating party.

Legal Requirements

We disclose information when required by law, government request, court order, or regulatory requirement. We disclose the minimum information necessary to comply with legal obligations. We may disclose information to protect our legal rights or respond to threats to safety or property.

Law Enforcement

We cooperate with law enforcement when legally required. We never voluntarily provide passenger information to authorities without legal process.

Business Transfers

If Limo4All Transportation is acquired, merged, or assets are sold, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We notify you of any ownership changes and your privacy rights remain protected.

Aggregated and Anonymized Data

We may share aggregated, anonymized data about transportation patterns, service usage, and market trends without identifying you. This data helps improve transportation services industry-wide.

Consent-Based Sharing

Beyond the above, we never share your information without your explicit consent.

### 4. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

Encryption

All personal information transmitted over the internet is encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols. Payment information is encrypted using PCI-DSS standards.

Access Controls

We limit employee access to personal information to those who need it for their job functions. All employees sign confidentiality agreements. Contractors and vendors are subject to similar restrictions.

Secure Storage

We store data on secured servers with firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and regular security updates. Backups are encrypted and stored securely.

Regular Security Audits

We conduct regular security assessments, vulnerability testing, and penetration testing. We maintain security certifications and comply with industry standards.

Password Protection

We encourage strong passwords and provide two-factor authentication options for account security.

What We Cannot Guarantee

While we implement robust security measures, no system is completely impenetrable. We cannot guarantee absolute security against sophisticated attacks, though we maintain industry-leading protection.

### 5. Your Data Rights

You have rights regarding your personal information. We respect and facilitate these rights.

Access

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you. Contact us with your request and we'll provide your data in a standard format within 30 days.

Correction

You have the right to correct inaccurate information. Update your profile directly in our app/website or contact us to request corrections.

Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your personal information. We'll delete your information except where we're legally required to retain it (for example, payment records for accounting purposes). Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

Portability

You have the right to receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format. We'll provide your information in standard format compatible with other services.

Opt-Out

You have the right to opt-out of non-essential communications. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails by clicking the unsubscribe link or contacting us.

Restrict Processing

You can request that we restrict how we process your information. We'll honor reasonable restrictions while maintaining necessary functionality for service delivery.

Objection

You can object to certain uses of your information. We'll honor objections to non-essential processing.

### 6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years old. If a parent or guardian believes we've collected information about a child under 13, please contact us immediately and we'll delete the information.

Teenagers (13-18) may use our services with parental consent. We collect minimal information from younger users and apply enhanced privacy protections.

### 7. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies. You control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies maintain your login session, process bookings, and maintain security. These cookies cannot be disabled without breaking functionality.

Performance Cookies

Performance cookies track site usage, page views, and performance metrics. This helps us improve our website. You can disable these without affecting core functionality.

Marketing Cookies

Marketing cookies track your interests across websites to deliver targeted advertising. You can disable marketing cookies through browser settings or opt-out links.

Third-Party Cookies

Third parties including analytics providers and advertising networks may set cookies on our site. We don't control third-party cookies but you can manage them through your browser.

Do Not Track

Some browsers include Do Not Track features. We respect Do Not Track signals though we cannot guarantee all third parties honor these preferences.

### 8. Third-Party Links and Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We're not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their privacy policies before sharing information.

We may integrate third-party services including payment processors, mapping services, and analytics platforms. These third parties have their own privacy policies. Using their services means you accept their privacy terms.

### 9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide services and fulfill legal obligations.

Active Accounts

Information for active accounts is retained to provide ongoing service and support. You can request account deletion at any time.

Completed Transactions

Payment records and transaction history are retained for accounting, tax, and legal purposes (typically 7 years as required by law).

Communications

Email and communication records are retained for customer support purposes (typically 2-3 years) unless longer retention is legally required.

Marketing Data

Marketing communication records are retained until you opt-out, then deleted within 30 days.

Deleted Accounts

When you request account deletion, personal information is deleted within 30 days except where legal obligations require longer retention.

### 10. International Data Transfers

Limo4All Transportation operates primarily in Canada. If you're accessing our services from outside Canada, be aware that your information may be processed in Canada where data protection laws may differ from your home country. By using our services, you consent to data transfer to Canada.

### 11. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. We'll notify you of material changes by email or prominent website notice at least 30 days before the change takes effect.

Your continued use of our services after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

### 13. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you're a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

Right to Know: You can request what personal information we collect and how it's used.

Right to Delete: You can request deletion of personal information we've collected.

Right to Opt-Out: You can opt-out of selling or sharing your personal information (we don't sell data but you can control processing).

Right to Non-Discrimination: We won't discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise California rights, contact our Privacy Officer with your request.

### 14. European GDPR Rights (International Users)

If you're in the European Union, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:

Legal Basis: We process information based on service delivery (contract), legal compliance, legitimate business interests, and your consent.

Data Protection Officer: Contact our Privacy Officer for GDPR-related inquiries.

Right to Lodge Complaint: You can file complaints with your national data protection authority.

Standard Contractual Clauses: International data transfers are protected by EU-approved standard contractual clauses.
