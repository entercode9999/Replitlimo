# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions About Limo4All Transportation

Have questions about our services, booking process, pricing, or company? Find answers to common questions below. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our team anytime.

### Booking & Reservations

How do I book Limo4All Transportation?

Booking is easy and fast. You can book online at our website, through our mobile app, or by calling our team directly. All methods take just minutes and you'll receive instant confirmation.

How far in advance should I book?

You can book anytime from weeks in advance to immediately before service. For special events like weddings or corporate functions, we recommend booking 2-3 months ahead to ensure availability and allow time for planning.

Can I book same-day transportation?

Absolutely. Our 24/7 service accommodates last-minute bookings. Call or use our app immediately and we'll coordinate your ride right away based on driver availability.

What information do I need to book?

Provide your pickup location, destination, preferred date and time, and vehicle preference. Any special requests help us customize your experience.

Can I modify my reservation?

Yes. Changes to date, time, pickup location, destination, or vehicle type can be made online, through the app, or by phone. Make changes as early as possible.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel more than two hours before pickup with no charge. Cancellations within two hours may incur a $30-$50 fee. No-shows are charged the full ride cost. Emergency cancellations are handled with understanding.

### Pricing & Payments

How is pricing determined?

Pricing is based on vehicle type, distance, duration, time of day, and demand. Our transparent system provides quotes before booking so you know exact pricing.

Why is pricing transparent?

We believe customers deserve to know pricing before committing. No surge charges, no hidden fees, no surprises. Your quoted price is final.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), debit cards, digital payment methods (Apple Pay, Google Pay), and corporate billing arrangements.

Can I use corporate billing?

Yes. Corporate accounts receive customized billing including consolidated monthly invoicing. Contact our corporate team to set up a corporate account.

Is tipping included in the quoted price?

Tipping is never required but always appreciated. Your quoted price is your final cost. Tips can be added in cash or card when your ride is complete.

Do you charge for waiting time?

Excessive waiting time (longer than 10 minutes) after service completion may result in additional charges. Drivers cannot wait indefinitely; communicate with your driver if delays occur.

Do you offer corporate accounts?
