### Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book my private car service to the airport?

We recommend booking at least 2 hours in advance, though we accommodate same-day bookings based on driver availability. For major holidays or peak travel times, booking further in advance ensures optimal availability.

Do you offer airport transportation for larger groups?

Absolutely. Our fleet ranges from luxury sedans to full-size charter buses accommodating 3 to 35 passengers. Whether traveling solo or with a large group, we have the perfect vehicle.

What if I need help with my luggage?

Our professional chauffeurs assist with luggage as part of our service. We load and unload your bags with courtesy and care, making your airport experience seamless.

Are your private airport transportation services shared or private?

We offer both options. Private service ensures dedicated transportation for your group, while shared options provide cost savings for single travelers or small groups going to the same destination.

Do you service all Toronto area airports?

Yes, we service Toronto Pearson International (YYZ), Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport (YTZ), Hamilton International Airport (YHM), and Buffalo Niagara International (BUF), plus regional airports throughout Ontario.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, digital payments, corporate billing, and cash. Payment is due upon service completion.

Can I track my driver in real time?

Yes, our online booking system allows you to receive confirmation and updates about your driver arrival time via phone or email.

What if my flight is delayed?

We recommend contacting us immediately if your flight status changes. Let us know about any delays and we'll adjust your pickup time accordingly. You'll receive notification of any changes via phone or email.
